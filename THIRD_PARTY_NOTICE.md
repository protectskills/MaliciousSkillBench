# Third-Party Notice

MaliciousSkillBench consolidates public Agent Skill resources for defensive
research. Third-party Skill artifacts included or referenced by this project
are **not** relicensed under Apache-2.0 or CC BY 4.0.

Those artifacts retain their respective upstream terms and attribution
requirements. Apache-2.0 applies to MaliciousSkillBench-authored software.
CC BY 4.0 applies to MaliciousSkillBench-authored metadata, derived
annotations, taxonomy mappings, and split manifests, unless otherwise noted.

The registry below uses the public source records in
`metadata/source_registry.csv`. It is an attribution pointer, not a license
audit. Package-level source artifacts, where present under `packages/`,
retain their respective upstream terms.
